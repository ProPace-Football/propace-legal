ProPace

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

1. About ProPace

ProPace is a football training application that helps players organise and complete individual, position-matched training sessions and track their progress over time.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ProPace collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how you can request deletion of your account and associated data.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ProPace, we may collect:

  • Email address and authentication credentials used to sign up or sign in.
  • Training profile details you provide: display name, age, playing position, experience level, preferred training days and reminder settings.
  • Training activity: which sessions you complete and when, used to calculate your streak and progress.
  • Subscription status processed through Google Play. We receive a record that your subscription is active; we never receive or store your card details.
  • Technical information required to operate and secure the service.

3. How We Use Information

ProPace uses collected information to:

  • Create and authenticate your account.
  • Build and deliver your training programme and sessions.
  • Save and sync your training progress and streaks across devices.
  • Send the optional training reminders you choose.
  • Manage your ProPace Pro subscription entitlement.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.
  • Improve the reliability of the application.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Service Providers

ProPace uses third-party services to provide parts of the application:

  • Supabase for authentication and backend infrastructure (your account and training data are stored here).
  • Google Play for Android distribution and subscription purchases.

Training reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. These services process information according to their own privacy policies and the configuration used by ProPace.

5. Data Security

Access to your data is protected by authentication and row-level security so that only you can read your personal records. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Account information and training data are retained while your account is active, or where retention is otherwise necessary for legitimate operational, security, legal, or regulatory purposes.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your ProPace account and associated personal data at any time, with or without the app installed.

Use the ProPace account deletion page to verify your email and submit a deletion request. You can also delete your account from inside the app under Profile → Delete account.

Deleting your account permanently removes your authentication record and cascades to your profile and training data. Some records (for example a minimal deletion audit log) may be retained where necessary for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

ProPace is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 18 you should use the app with a parent or guardian's permission. We only process information required to provide the services described in this policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ProPace's functionality, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page. Where required, we will also notify you in the app.
